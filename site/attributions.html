Data sources and attributions

Merge's maps and area names are built on open data. Thank you to everyone who makes it.

Area names

Merge labels each approximate area with a neighbourhood or city name, using these boundaries. Their licences don't require attribution, but we credit them anyway.

DataSourceLicence
Neighborhood boundaries (San Francisco) DataSF Analysis Neighborhoods PDDL 1.0
City limits US Census Bureau TIGER/Line 2025 Public domain
East Bay neighborhoods (Alameda, Oakland, Berkeley) Hand-drawn by Merge (download) CC0 1.0

The East Bay outlines are approximate by design: they label a circle about half a mile wide, not an exact place.
