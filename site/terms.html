1. The pilot

Merge is in a free, invite-only pilot for commuters travelling between the East Bay and San Francisco. The Merge team adds each tester by hand. Pilot software can have bugs, features may change or be removed, and we may end the pilot at any time. When the pilot ends, we'll tell testers by email before deleting accounts, as described in the Privacy Policy.

2. Who can use Merge

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must be invited to the pilot, and use only your own account.
  • The information you give us must be accurate, including your name, commute and vehicle.
  • If you drive, you must hold a valid driver's license, and the car you drive must be registered and insured as the law requires. The Merge team checks drivers' licenses and insurance in person or on a video call. [Whether an unchecked driver can be matched or confirm a ride as the driver]

3. Merge doesn't provide transportation

Merge is a way for commuters who already make the same trip to find each other and agree to share a ride. Merge doesn't provide transportation, doesn't own or operate vehicles, and doesn't employ or contract drivers. Drivers aren't Merge's employees or agents. Each ride is an arrangement between the members taking it, and Merge doesn't control how a ride is driven.

A "License & insurance checked by Merge" badge means the Merge team looked at that driver's license and insurance once, in person or on a video call. It isn't a background check, a driving record check, or a guarantee of anyone's safety or conduct.

Confirm this position keeps Merge a free carpool outside California's transportation network company (TNC) rules.

4. No payment between riders

Merge is free during the pilot. Don't ask for, offer, or accept money or anything else of value for a ride arranged through Merge, including tips, gas money or tolls.

Confirm whether cost-sharing (for example splitting tolls) should stay prohibited.

5. Requests, rides and cancellations

  • Every new match starts with one ride on one date. Recurring rides happen only if you both choose them.
  • Drivers reply to requests by 8 PM (Pacific time) the evening before the ride, or the request expires. A declined request looks the same as an expired one.
  • When confirming, the driver sets the exact pickup spot inside the passenger's pickup area. If the spot doesn't work for you, cancel.
  • Cancel by 9 PM (Pacific time) the evening before. You can still cancel later, but please do it as early as you can: someone may be counting on you. Late cancellations are recorded privately, with no penalty during the pilot.
  • If a ride didn't happen, either rider can mark it within 24 hours.
  • Passengers may bring one folding scooter, folded within 120 × 50 × 60 cm and 20 kg (44 lb). The driver decides whether to accept it when confirming.

6. HOV lanes and travel time

Merge doesn't promise that a carpool will qualify for HOV or carpool lanes, toll discounts, or any time savings. It's up to the driver to follow lane and occupancy rules.

7. How to behave

When you use Merge, and during rides arranged through it:

  • Follow traffic laws. Drivers: don't drive impaired or distracted, and don't use the app while driving. Everyone wears a seatbelt.
  • Be on time, and tell the other rider through the app if your plans change.
  • Treat other members with respect. No harassment, threats, discrimination, or unwanted contact.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share another member's contact details, pickup spot or other information, and don't use Merge to find out where someone lives or works.
  • Don't carry anything illegal or dangerous.
  • Don't misuse Merge: no fake accounts, no scraping or collecting other members' information, no interfering with the service, and no security testing against other members' accounts. Report security problems privately as described on the Support page.

8. Safety

In immediate danger? Call 911.

You decide whether to take or give a ride, and you can cancel or leave if you don't feel safe. To report a safety concern, use "Report a safety concern" in the app or write to safety@[domain]. The Merge team checks reports daily and replies within 24 hours. You can block anyone; you'll stop appearing to each other.

9. Suspension and removal

We may suspend or remove anyone from the pilot, for example for breaking these Terms or for safety reasons. When we do, that person is hidden from other members, their pending requests are withdrawn, their upcoming rides are cancelled, and their Crews end. You can stop using Merge and delete your account at any time.
