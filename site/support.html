Support

Help with Merge, the free commuter carpool pilot for East Bay to San Francisco trips.

In immediate danger? Call 911. Merge can't send help.

Safety concerns

  • After a ride, use Report a safety concern on the post-ride screen. Safety reports are separate from ride feedback, and the person you report never sees them.
  • The Merge team checks reports daily and replies within 24 hours.
  • You can block anyone. You'll stop appearing to each other, and they aren't told.
  • We may remove people from the pilot for safety reasons. See the Terms of Use.

Common questions

How do I join the pilot?

The pilot is invite-only, for people 18 and older. The Merge team adds each tester by hand. If you see "This email isn't on the Merge pilot list yet" when signing in, that email address hasn't been added. Check you typed the address you were invited with, or contact support@[domain].

My sign-in code didn't arrive

  • Check your spam or junk folder for an email titled "Your Merge sign-in code".
  • Codes expire after 10 minutes. Ask for a new code and use the newest one.
  • If it still doesn't arrive, wait a few minutes before trying again, then contact support.

Does Merge track my location?

No. Other members never see your location. During a ride, location is used only on the phone to notice arrival, and Merge's servers receive only "arrived" and the time. Others see your commute only as an approximate area about half a mile wide. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Merge cost anything?

No. Merge is free, and riders don't pay each other, not even for gas or tolls.

How do I cancel a ride?

Cancel in the app by 9 PM (Pacific time) the evening before the ride. You can still cancel later, but please do it as early as you can.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, or by asking us. See Delete your account.

Security reports

Please don't report security problems in a public issue. Report them privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, as described in the project's security policy.

About Merge

Merge is open source. You can read the code and the product plan on GitHub. Map and area-name data sources are listed on Data sources.
