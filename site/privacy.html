Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Effective date]

This draft describes the pilot as designed. Some features it covers (ride messages, contact sharing, push notifications, ride mode, crash reporting) aren't built yet. Check each section against the app before publishing.

The short version

  • Other members never see your exact home, work or pickup point. Before a ride is confirmed they see your first name and last initial, your role, an approximate area about half a mile wide, your ride preferences and verification flags.
  • Merge doesn't track your location. During a ride, location is used only on the phone to notice arrival, and the server receives just "arrived" and the time.
  • Nobody is told who said no. Your post-ride feedback and your safety reports are never shown to the other person.
  • No ads, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal information.
  • You can delete your account in the app or ask us to delete it.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Merge mobile app and these web pages during the pilot. The pilot is invite-only: the Merge team adds each tester by hand, and testers must be 18 or older.

3. What we collect

We collect only what Merge needs to match commuters, arrange rides and keep members safe.

Account and profile

  • Email address, to sign you in with a 6-digit code and to send account emails. The code expires after 10 minutes.
  • Display name, which you choose. Other members see only your first name and last initial.
  • Role (driver, passenger or both), your ride preferences (quiet ride, smoke-free) and whether you've turned on discovery. Discovery is off until you turn it on.
  • Account dates: when you joined, confirmed your email and last signed in.
  • Driver check: if you drive, the date the Merge team checked your driver's license and insurance. We look at them in person or on a live video call and keep no copies.
  • Account status: if we remove someone from the pilot for a safety reason, we record when.

Commute

  • Start and end points that you place as pins on the map. We don't use address search, so we never receive your street address.
  • Approximate areas: for each point, the server picks and stores a circle about half a mile wide that contains it, labelled with a neighbourhood or city name from public boundary data. Other members see the area, never the point.
  • Schedule and flexibility: the days you commute, your departure time and time zone, how flexible your departure is, and the longest detour you'll accept.
  • Whether you bring a folding scooter.

Vehicle (drivers)

  • Make, model, year, colour, licence plate, passenger seats, and whether your trunk fits a folded scooter.

Ride requests and rides

  • Requests: who asked whom, for which date, and the status.
  • Rides: the driver and passenger, date, pickup time, the exact pickup spot and note the driver sets when confirming, and the ride's status.
  • Ride events: when the driver tapped "Picked up", when the ride arrived (a time only, never a location), and whether either rider marked that it didn't happen.
  • Cancellations: who cancelled and when, including late cancellations. Late cancellations are recorded privately, with no penalty during the pilot.
  • Post-ride feedback: how the ride went and whether you'd ride together again. Only you and the Merge team can see it.
  • Ride Again and Commute Crews: whether you and another member are connected, and the days and times of a Crew you agree to.

Messages, contact details and notifications

  • Ride messages you send to the other rider once a ride is confirmed.
  • Contact details you choose to share: a phone number, an email address, or both, that you type in. Merge doesn't verify phone numbers. We also record who offered to share with whom.
  • Push notification tokens that let us send notifications to your phone.

Safety

  • Blocks: who you've blocked. The person you block isn't told.
  • Safety reports: who reported whom, the ride, the category, what you wrote, and the Merge team's notes. Reports are stored separately from ride feedback.

Technical information

  • Server logs: when the app talks to our servers, they log your IP address, device and app details (user agent), what was requested, the result and the time.
  • Sign-in records: a security log of sign-ins, with your email address and IP address, and the session that keeps you signed in.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report with the error, device model, operating system and app version. Crash reports are scrubbed of personal details and don't include your IP address or user ID.

4. Location

  • Your commute pins are precise. You place them yourself; we don't use GPS for them. Only you and the Merge team can see them.
  • Ride mode uses location on the phone only. When the driver taps "Picked up", Merge starts using the phone's location to notice when the ride reaches its destination area. It keeps running in the background until arrival, with the system's location indicator (iOS) or a "Ride in progress" notification (Android) showing. Merge asks only for "while using the app" permission, never "Always", and doesn't use background location permission or geofencing.
  • The server never receives coordinates from ride mode. It receives only that the ride arrived, and when. Location stops when the ride ends.
  • No live tracking. Other members never see your location, during a ride or otherwise.
  • The map. To draw map tiles, your phone asks our map provider for the part of the map on screen. The provider sees your IP address and that map area (see section 7).

If you don't allow location, rides still work: a ride completes automatically after its expected trip time.

6. How we use your information

  • To run Merge: sign you in, match commutes, arrange and confirm rides, show you what you need for a ride, and send ride notifications.
  • To keep members safe: review safety reports, enforce blocks, check drivers, and remove people from the pilot when needed.
  • To keep Merge secure and working: prevent abuse (for example by limiting how many requests someone can send), investigate problems and fix crashes.
  • To contact you about your account and the pilot, including changes to these pages and the end of the pilot.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't build marketing profiles, and we don't sell or share your personal information with anyone for their own use.

8. What we don't collect

  • Payment or financial information. Merge is free, and riders don't pay each other.
  • Location history or live tracking.
  • Street addresses or address searches.
  • Copies of driver's licenses, insurance, or other ID documents.
  • Gender. There's no women-only option in the pilot.
  • Profile photos, contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or health data.
  • Advertising IDs, analytics or advertising SDKs, or cookies. These pages set no cookies and load nothing from other websites.
  • Phone sign-in or SMS. Sign-in is by email only.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your information in the app: your name, role, commute, vehicle and preferences.
  • Turn off discovery at any time to stop appearing to other members.
  • Block someone so you never appear to each other.
  • Stop or remove contact sharing at any time.
  • Turn off notifications or location in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us to delete it.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete something, by writing to privacy@[domain] from the email address on your account.

California residents

You can review and ask us to change your information as described above. We'll post any change to this policy on this page with a new effective date. Merge doesn't track you across other websites or apps, so it does nothing different when your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal. We don't sell or share personal information.

11. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Our database denies access by default, and each member can read only what the rules in section 5 allow. Exact points are never sent to other members' phones. Your session is stored on your phone until you sign out. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a security problem, please report it privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting.

12. Age

Merge is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact privacy@[domain] and we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above and tell testers by email before the change takes effect.
