Merge

Find your way together. Merge is a free commuter carpool app for East Bay to San Francisco trips over the Bay Bridge, starting with a small pilot in Alameda.

The pilot is invite-only. The Merge team adds each tester by hand, and you need to be 18 or older to take part. Every new match starts with one ride on one date, and you only keep riding together if you both want to.
