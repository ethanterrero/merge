Delete your Merge account

You can delete your account for the Merge carpool app at any time, in the app or by asking us. Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Merge and sign in.
  2. On the Discover screen, tap the Account button (the person icon at the top).
  3. Tap Delete account, read what will be deleted, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out.

Ask us to delete it

If you can't use the app, for example because you've uninstalled it or changed phones:

  1. Email privacy@[domain] from the email address you use to sign in to Merge, with the subject "Delete my Merge account".
  2. We'll reply to that address to confirm the request is yours. We only act on requests we can match to the account's email address.
  3. We'll delete the account within [number of days] and email you when it's done.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write to us anyway and say so, and we'll work out with you how to confirm it's your account.

What's deleted

  • Your profile: name, role, ride preferences, discovery setting, and the record of any driver check.
  • Your email address and sign-in records.
  • Your commutes, including your pinned points and approximate areas, and your vehicles.
  • Your post-ride feedback and your Ride Again connections.
  • Your pending requests, and any other request that didn't lead to a ride.
  • Blocks you made, and blocks others made against you.
  • Ride messages you wrote, contact details you shared, contact-sharing offers, and your push notification tokens.

We don't keep a copy or a scrambled ("hashed") version of your email address. To come back to the pilot, the Merge team would need to add you again.

Server logs (IP address, device details, requests) Not deleted early. [Supabase plan log retention], then they expire.

Crash reports They don't include your user ID or IP address, so they aren't linked to your account. 30 days, then they expire.

Contact details you shared that another member already saved on their own phone can't be pulled back.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app you can edit or delete your commute and vehicle, turn off discovery, and stop sharing contact details. To ask us to delete anything else, write to privacy@[domain].
